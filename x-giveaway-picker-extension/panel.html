Eligibility (every switch on is required)

Follow checks may not be reliable for multiple accounts. A follow that cannot be confirmed is never used to remove anyone: that winner is shown as needs review with a link to check by hand. Winners with a needs-review follow get a Verify followage button. For multiple accounts, it cannot auto-reverify followage.

Draw settings

Same seed + same pools and verification outcomes produce the same draw. Picked winners get a fresh live re-check of every active rule before they count as verified.
